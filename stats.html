Historical Directory Conventions

In 19th-century British street directories, women were only recorded when they were the recognized legal head of household (typically as widows) or proprietors of independent retail shops and trades.

The most frequent female vocational entries include dressmakers, milliners, grocers, confectioners, teachers, and boarding-house keepers.
